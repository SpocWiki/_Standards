
# [[Figueiredo,Carolina]] 


**Carolina Figueiredo** is a theoretical physicist 
specializing in high-energy particle physics and quantum field theory. 
2026 She will complete a prestigious junior fellowship at Harvard University 
and then begin her faculty role at UC Berkeley.  
  
Figueiredo is known for simplifying extremely complicated physics principles. 
She has identified new mathematical properties of the S-matrix, 
which is key to calculating probabilities of what happens 
when subatomic particles collide at near-light speeds.  
  
Figueiredo earned her Ph.D. from Princeton University this year, 
and she has already made major waves in her field. 

The Breakthrough Prize Foundation awarded Figueiredo its 
inaugural Vera Rubin New Frontiers Prize in April. 

Her work proved that three particle physics theories believed to be disparate 
were, in fact, linked through an underlying geometric structure. 

NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang and tech investor Yuri Milner presented the award to Figueiredo. 
Portuguese President António José Seguro even [congratulated her(link is external)](https://www.instagram.com/p/DXT9D89DTSp/), 
calling Figueiredo “an inspiration to Portugal.”
