# [[PKM(Personal_Knowledge_Management)]] 

#is_/same_as :: [[WD~Personal_Knowledge_Management,2034224]]

## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> **Personal knowledge management** (PKM) is a process of collecting information 
> that a person uses to gather, classify, store, search, retrieve and share knowledge 
> in their daily activities and the way in which these processes support work activities. 
> 
> It is a response to the idea that knowledge workers 
> need to be responsible for their own growth and learning. 
> 
> It is a bottom-up approach to knowledge management (KM).
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal%20knowledge%20management)
