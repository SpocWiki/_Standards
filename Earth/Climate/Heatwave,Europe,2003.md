# [[Heatwave,Europe,2003]] 

#is_/same_as :: [[WD~2003_European_heatwave,1136557]]

## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> The 2003 European heat wave saw the hottest summer recorded in Europe since at least 1540. Spain, France, and Italy were hit especially hard. 
> The heat wave led to health crises in several countries 
> and combined with drought to create a crop shortfall in parts of Southern Europe. 
> The death toll has been estimated at more than 70,000.
> 
> The predominant heat was recorded in July and August, 
> partly a result of the western European seasonal lag 
> from the maritime influence of the Atlantic warm waters 
> in combination with hot continental air and strong southerly winds.
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2003%20European%20heatwave)
