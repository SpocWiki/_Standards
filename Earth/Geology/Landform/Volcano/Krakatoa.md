## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> **Krakatoa**, also transcribed Krakatau, is a caldera in the Sunda Strait 
> between the islands of Java and Sumatra in the Indonesian province of Lampung. 
> 
> The caldera is part of a volcanic island group (Krakatoa archipelago) comprising four islands. 
> Two of them are known as Lang and Verlaten; 
> another, Rakata, is the only remnant of an island, also called Krakatoa, 
> mostly destroyed by an eruption in 1883 which created the caldera.
> 
> In 1927, a fourth island, Anak Krakatoa, or "Child of Krakatoa", 
> emerged from the caldera formed in 1883. 
> 
> There has been new eruptive activity since the late 20th century, 
> with a large collapse causing the 2018 Sunda Strait tsunami. 
> 
> A new cinder cone eventually grew out of its collapse scar from eruptions in the 2020s.
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krakatoa)
