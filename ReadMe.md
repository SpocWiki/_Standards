This Article is only a Stub. 

The git Repository https://github.com/SpocWiki/_Standards 
should be checked out into a sub-folder named `_Standards` next to this File. 

Beware though, that this can considerably increase 
the total size and depth of your wiki.

 On iPhone and Android you need a [[IT/Algorithms/Code-Versioning/Git|Git]] Application for this. 

In addition to basic Standards, 
you can also download extensive geographic and historic Data, 
but be aware to not exceed ca. 50_000 Nodes, 
because Obsidian becomes slow or even dies on trying to index large batches.
