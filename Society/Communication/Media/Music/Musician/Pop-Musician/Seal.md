
## #has_/text_of_/abstract

> Seal (born Henry Olusegun Adeola Samuel, 19 February 1963) is a British singer and
> songwriter.
>
> He rose to prominence in 1990 with "Killer", a collaboration with producer
> Adamski, followed by his self-titled debut solo album Seal (1991), which
> included the hit "Crazy". His second album, also self-titled and often
> distinguished as Seal II (1994), included "Kiss from a Rose", which won three
> Grammy Awards in 1996 including Record of the Year and Song of the Year after
> being featured on the soundtrack of Batman Forever.
>
> His subsequent studio albums include Human Being (1998), Seal IV (2003), and
> the covers albums Soul (2008) and Soul 2 (2011), the former going platinum in
> the US. He was married to model Heidi Klum from 2005 to 2012.
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seal%20(musician))
