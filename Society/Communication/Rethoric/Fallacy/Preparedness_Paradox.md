## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> The **Preparedness Paradox** is the proposition that 
> if a society or individual acts effectively to mitigate a potential disaster 
> such as a pandemic, natural disaster or other catastrophe so that it causes less harm, 
> the avoided danger will be perceived as having been much less serious 
> because of the limited damage actually caused. 
> 
> The paradox is the incorrect perception that there had been no need for careful preparation 
> as there was little harm, although in reality the limitation of the harm was **due to** preparation. 
> 
> Several cognitive biases can consequently hamper proper preparation for future risks.
>
