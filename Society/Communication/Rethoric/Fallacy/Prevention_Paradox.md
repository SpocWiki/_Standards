# [[Prevention_Paradox]]

#is_/same_as :: [[WD~Prevention_Paradox,7242394]] 
#is_/similar_to :: [[Preparedness_Paradox]] 

## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> The **Prevention paradox** describes the situation where the majority of cases of a disease 
> come from a population at low or moderate risk of that disease, 
> and only a minority of cases come from the high risk population (of the same disease). 
> 
> This is because the number of people at high risk is small. 
> The prevention paradox was first formally described in 1981 
> by the epidemiologist Geoffrey Rose. 
>
> Especially in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic, 
> the term "prevention paradox" was also used to describe the apparent paradox of 
> people questioning steps to prevent the spread of the pandemic 
> because the prophesied spread did not occur. 
> This however is instead an example of a self-defeating prophecy or a [[Preparedness_Paradox]].
>
