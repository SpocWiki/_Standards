## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> **Hillbilly Elegy**: A Memoir of a Family and Culture in Crisis 
> is a 2016 memoir by JD Vance about the Appalachian values of his family from Kentucky 
> and the socioeconomic problems of his hometown of Middletown, Ohio, 
> where his mother's parents moved when they were young. 
> 
> It was adapted into the 2020 film Hillbilly Elegy, 
> directed by Ron Howard and starring Glenn Close and Amy Adams.
> 
> Later, Vance progressed in politics, 
> becoming a senator from Ohio in 2023 and the vice president of the United States in 2025.
>
