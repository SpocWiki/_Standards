## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> Life skills have been defined as "abilities for adaptive and positive 
> behavior that enable humans to deal effectively with the demands and challenges of life". 
> 
> This concept is also termed as psychosocial competency. 
> The subject varies greatly depending on social norms and community expectations 
> but skills that function for well-being 
> and aid individuals to develop into active and productive members of their communities 
> are considered as life skills.
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life%20skills)


## Core skills

The [World Health Organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Health_Organization "World Health Organization") in 1999 identified the following core cross-cultural areas of life skills:

- [decision-making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision-making "Decision-making") and [problem-solving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Problem-solving "Problem-solving");
- [creative thinking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_thinking "Creative thinking") (_see also:_ [lateral thinking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lateral_thinking "Lateral thinking")) and [critical thinking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_thinking "Critical thinking");
- [communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communication "Communication") and [interpersonal skills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interpersonal_skills "Interpersonal skills");
- [self-awareness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-awareness "Self-awareness") and [empathy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empathy "Empathy");
- [assertiveness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assertiveness "Assertiveness") and [equanimity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equanimity "Equanimity"); and
- [resilience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_resilience "Psychological resilience") and [coping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coping_\(psychology\) "Coping (psychology)") with emotions and coping with [stress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_stress "Psychological stress").

UNICEF listed similar skills and related categories in its 2012 report.
