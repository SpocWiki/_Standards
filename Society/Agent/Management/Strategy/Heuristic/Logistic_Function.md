# [[Logistic_Function]] 
> The logistic function or logistic curve is a common S-shaped curve (sigmoid curve) 
>
> The logistic function finds applications in a range of fields, including biology (especially ecology), biomathematics, chemistry, demography, economics, geoscience, mathematical psychology, probability, sociology, political science, linguistics, statistics, and artificial neural networks. 
> 
> A generalization of the logistic function is the [[Hyperbolastic_Function]] of type I.
>
> The standard logistic function, is sometimes simply called the sigmoid. 
> It is also sometimes called the expit, being the inverse of the logit.
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logistic%20function)
