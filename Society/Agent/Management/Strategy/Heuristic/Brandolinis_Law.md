# [[Brandolinis_Law]] 

#has_/created_/quote :: The amount of energy needed to refute bullshit is an order of magnitude bigger than that needed to produce it. 

#is_/similar_to :: An idiot can create more bullshit than you could ever hope to refute 
#is_/similar_to :: A lie can travel halfway around the world before the truth can get its boots on.
#is_/similar_to :: Falsehood _flies_, and truth comes _limping_ after it (Jonathan Swift, The Examiner)
#is_/similar_to :: In very few words they can announce a half-truth; and in order to demonstrate that it is incomplete, we are obliged to have recourse to long and dry dissertations. (Frédéric Bastiat, Economic Sophisms)

## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> **Brandolini's law** (or the bullshit asymmetry principle) is an Internet adage 
> coined in 2013 by Italian programmer Alberto Brandolini. 
> 
> It compares the considerable effort of debunking misinformation 
> to the relative ease of creating it in the first place. 
> 
> The adage states:
>
> **The amount of energy needed to refute bullshit** 
> **is an order of magnitude bigger than that needed to produce it.**
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandolini's%20law)
