## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> A **Prosumer** is an individual who both consumes and produces. 
> The term is a portmanteau of the words producer and consumer. 
> 
> Research has identified six types of prosumers: 
> - DIY prosumers, 
> - self-service prosumers, 
> - customizing prosumers, 
> - collaborative prosumers, 
> - monetised prosumers, and 
> - economic prosumers.
>
