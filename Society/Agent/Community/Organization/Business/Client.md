# [[Client]] 

## #has_/text_of_/abstract  

> In business, commerce, and economics, a client is a person 
> who receives __advice or services__ 
> from a professional, such as a lawyer or a health care provider. 
> 
> Clients differ from [[Customer]]s in that 
> customers are thought of as "one-time buyers" while 
> clients can be seen as "long-term recipients", 
> and customers buy goods as well as services.
>
> [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Client%20(business))
